Questions worth asking.

  • How much reaches direct nutrition? In this fund, 100% of disbursed donations went directly to food. Delivery fees across all 22 cycles were sponsored by Phêrô Nguyễn, and administrative deficits were topped up out-of-pocket by admins.
  • Can delivery be verified independently? Every monthly batch includes a public photo album index with STT markers linking each delivered portion to the recipient shelter.
  • How is emergency resilience maintained? Beyond monthly dry food and wet food distributions, the central fund maintains an unallocated reserve balance for emergency veterinary rescue.
