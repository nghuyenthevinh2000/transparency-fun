TAKE IT INTO THE REAL WORLD

Questions worth asking.

  • Why is chronological playback essential for transparency? Single summary totals obscure whether aid arrived steadily or in irregular bursts. Stepping through 22 cycles proves that cat shelters received steady monthly rations without gaps.
  • How do admin top-ups protect food deliveries? When monthly bulk food costs exceeded available fund distributions, administrators paid the difference out-of-pocket rather than reducing food rations for the cats.
  • How is proof preserved across years? Every cycle retains an immutable photo record index in Google Drive (#STT 1–20+), allowing donors to verify their specific gift reach the bowls.
