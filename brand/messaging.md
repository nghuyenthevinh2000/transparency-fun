# Brand messaging

## Core message

Transparency Fun helps curious people explore the details behind subjects they care about through interactive, inspectable reports.

## Motto

**Transparency, made fun to explore.**

This is the shared line for the directory and future report introductions. Pair it with **passion** in supporting copy: visitors follow what they care about, and the reports reward their curiosity. Do not imply every subject is lighthearted; “fun” describes the act of exploring, not the seriousness of the underlying story.

## Website hierarchy

- **Hero headline:** “Transparency, made fun.”
- **Supporting line:** “Transparency, made fun to explore. Follow your passion through interactive reports that reveal the stories behind the numbers.”
- **Action:** “Explore the reports.”
- **Proof:** Each published report has its own interactive page, shows assumptions and sources, and lets visitors follow the steps themselves.

## Use across reports

Start with the specific question each report answers. Then explain what visitors can explore and which claims are sourced, estimated, or illustrative. Keep the brand motto in shared touchpoints such as the directory and footer; don't repeat it as a substitute for a report's own subject and evidence.
