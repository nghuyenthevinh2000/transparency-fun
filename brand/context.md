# Brand context

- **Name:** Transparency Fun
- **What it is:** A growing directory of standalone interactive reports and infographics.
- **Who it serves:** Curious people who want to explore systems, donations, and decisions they care about rather than just read a static report.
- **Promise:** Make the steps, inputs, evidence, and uncertainties behind a report easier to see and question.
- **Positioning:** Transparency can be engaging to explore without losing its honesty. Fun is the experience of discovery; passion is the reason someone cares; exploration is how they make sense of it.
- **Voice:** Clear, curious, warm, and playful. Do not trivialize serious subjects or dress guesses up as facts.
- **Visual direction:** Open notebook. See [`../DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) for the shared design system.
